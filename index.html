Task 2 — Question Types & Structures
Type 1 · Discussion
"Discuss both views and give your opinion"
IntroParaphrase + your position
Body 1View A — the side you agree with less
Body 2View B — the side you lean toward
ConclusionRestate opinion + brief summary
Don't skip discussing one side — that kills Task Achievement.
Type 2 · Opinion
"To what extent do you agree or disagree?"
IntroParaphrase + clear stance
Body 1Reason 1 for your stance
Body 2Reason 2 — or concede opposing view, then counter it
ConclusionRestate stance
Pick a clear side. Sitting on the fence hurts your score.
Type 3 · Problem / Solution
"What are the causes? What are the solutions?"
IntroParaphrase + signal you'll cover both
Body 12 causes with explanation
Body 22 solutions linked to those causes
ConclusionBrief summary
Match solutions to causes — don't introduce new problems in Body 2.
Type 4 · Two-part question
"Why is this happening? Is this positive or negative?"
IntroParaphrase + signal you'll answer both parts
Body 1Answer part 1 fully
Body 2Answer part 2 fully
ConclusionBrief summary
Both parts need roughly equal space — don't neglect one.
Introduction Formula (all 4 types)
1Paraphrase the question — do not copy it word for word.
2State your position or what you will cover. That's it — 2 sentences only.
Never start with: "In this essay I will tell you about..." — it's filler and wastes words.
